# Merchant payment operations compared

19 November 2026 · Public product documentation · Design input for the independent dakim pay simulator

| Product | Who presents what and who confirms the payment | What happens when the answer is missing |
|---|---|---|
| Stripe Payment Intents | A merchant associates the customer's order with an intent. The intent can require customer action or wait for capture. The merchant backend can retrieve it and receive events as it changes. | Retry an eligible command with its original key and parameters within the provider's retention window. A webhook can arrive twice or out of order. Verify its signature and retrieve the original object before making a financial decision. [Intent lifecycle](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment_intents) · [Idempotent requests](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests) · [Webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks) |
| WeChat Pay Global v3 | In Native Payment, the merchant obtains an order-specific code URL and displays the QR for the shopper. In Quick Pay, the cashier scans the shopper's payment code and submits that transaction. | Quick Pay can return `USERPAYING`. Query the original order. Verify notifications and expected amount. An eligible Quick Pay revocation can close an unpaid order **or refund an already successful one**. Track its financial result before asking the shopper to pay again. [Native Payment](https://pay.wechatpay.cn/doc/global/v3/en/4012356795) · [Quick Pay](https://pay.wechatpay.cn/doc/global/v3/en/4012356443) · [Notifications](https://pay.wechatpay.cn/doc/global/v3/en/4012356834) · [Order revocation](https://pay.wechatpay.cn/doc/global/v3/en/4012356791) |
| Alipay Global In-store Payment | With a static merchant QR, the shopper enters the amount. A transaction QR ties a dynamic code to a particular checkout. In Barcode Payment, the shopper presents the payment code and the cashier scans it. | Follow the asynchronous outcome or query the merchant order. Bind the order and actual amount before releasing goods, especially when the shopper entered the amount. Apply the selected product's cancellation rules to the original order. [Merchant QR](https://global.alipay.com/product-detail.htm?bizCode=OPEN_OFFLINE_PAYMENT-Merchant) · [Transaction QR](https://global.alipay.com/product-detail.htm?bizCode=OPEN_OFFLINE_PAYMENT-Transaction) · [Barcode Payment](https://global.alipay.com/product-detail.htm?bizCode=OPEN_OFFLINE_PAYMENT-Barcode) · [Transaction flows](https://docs.alipayplus.com/alipayplus/alipayplus/transactionqrcode/main_flow) |

## Refunds and deposits

| Product | What finance can prove |
|---|---|
| Stripe | An original charge can have multiple partial refunds. A refund can remain pending or fail. For automatic payouts the itemized reconciliation report identifies the included payments, refunds, fees and disputes. Manual and instant payouts have different reconciliation arrangements. Finance still needs bank-side evidence of receipt. [Refund lifecycle](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds) · [Payout reconciliation](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/payout-reconciliation) |
| WeChat Pay Global v3 | Keep a merchant refund reference for each partial refund and use it to query completion after acceptance. Processing, successful, closed and abnormal outcomes require different handling. Match the merchant and wallet transaction and refund references, amounts, currency and fees against the statement. [Request a refund](https://pay.wechatpay.cn/doc/global/v3/en/4012356520) · [Query a refund](https://pay.wechatpay.cn/doc/global/v3/en/4012356521) · [Statement](https://pay.wechatpay.cn/doc/global/v3/en/4012356455) |
| Alipay Global In-store Payment | A partially refunded order can still show a successful sale. Check each refund's outcome. Compare the transaction file, which includes reversals and refunds, to the settlement file by funding cycle. A sale date alone does not determine its deposit. [Order and refund states](https://global.alipay.com/developer/helpcenter/detail?_route=sg&categoryId=67616&knowId=201602452385&sceneCode=AC_DEV) · [Transaction and settlement files](https://global.alipay.com/developer/helpcenter/detail?_route=sg&categoryId=50478&knowId=201602452430&sceneCode=AC_DEV) |

## Ranked engineering gaps

Priorities below reflect the financial exposure of Mimi's existing register. The provider descriptions above are source claims. The priorities and acceptance tests are dakim pay design decisions.

| Rank | Exposure | Decision and acceptance evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Mimi could receipt a capture while an original refund was unresolved. A fully refunded order with goods still at the till could also stay held indefinitely because finance lacked the individual movements. | Selected for this slice. Check the original acquirer's capture, dispute observation and every refund against dakim pay. Require an exact sum, the original references, physical custody and independent finance approval to resolve a full return. Trace the remaining fee to its bank entry. |
| 2 | A refund, dispute or bank return can arrive after a retail receipt or a previously matched deposit. The merchant's books can remain apparently balanced while a later debit goes unrecognized. | Next checkpoint. Ingest a synthetic post-receipt adjustment, preserve its original linkage and statement date, and reconcile its own debit through to a second bank entry. Hold missing or conflicting evidence for finance. |
| 3 | A shopper may still authorize while a cashier cancels or a QR expires. A blind retry can create a second debit. | Before enabling a wallet, build one order-bound merchant-presented flow. Test an expired or scanned-twice code, wrong amount, lost notification and late success. Then exercise consumer-presented code consumption, original-order query and a revoke racing success under an explicit simulator contract. |
| 4 | Scheme labels, simulator outcomes and sample fees cannot establish actual merchant eligibility, liability, support or bank funding. | Qualify each selected product with its contracted partner in the target market. Obtain actual code-handling, state, authentication, dispute, fee and settlement rules. Match an authorized pilot to independent bank records before enabling a live channel. |

The local reader and online checkout still use synthetic card instruments. Wallet QR acceptance, provider credentials and real settlement are future gates. The comparison uses public behavior as a benchmark. The data model, implementation and tests are local to this project.

The [post-receipt refund checkpoint](post-receipt-refund-checkpoint.md) covers the acquirer-initiated refund in the second row. It records the original and later bank entries independently. Disputes and returned bank transfers remain open.
