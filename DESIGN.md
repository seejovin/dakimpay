# dakim pay

## Merchant acceptance and settlement design

**Decision.** Establish dakim pay as an independent payment technology supplier. Mimi is its first merchant and the seller of the groceries. Mimi contracts directly with an acquirer for card acceptance and separately with dakim pay for software, supplied terminals and support. The acquirer pays Mimi’s verified bank account. dakim pay invoices Mimi separately. A second retailer can use the same service from its own POS.

**Status at design time.** The initial plan deferred Programme 7. The later [simulation implementation](README.md) exercises the first merchant, terminal and online channels locally. Live activation remains deferred. This is a proposed design dated 19 November 2026. The launch country, legal entities, currency, partner, terminal model, fee schedule, funding timetable, service hours and required authorizations are open decisions. The dollar example is synthetic. The first release assumes one market, one currency, one acquiring partner, an attended countertop terminal and partner-hosted online card checkout. Each sale has a final, fixed amount. Variable-weight online baskets enter checkout after weighing and substitutions are complete. A changed total requires a new customer-approved quote and resolution of any existing payment.

## Who owns what

| Party | Authority and operating duties | Evidence supplied to the next party |
|---|---|---|
| Mimi | Prices baskets, authorizes retail returns, manages inventory, issues retail receipts and fulfills orders. Selects its deposit account directly with the acquirer. Runs its own accounting and approves its own bank reconciliation. | Merchant order and tender references, expected amount and currency, refund authority, independent posted bank entry. |
| dakim pay | Runs the merchant API, terminal assignment and support, hosted-checkout integration, processor connector, transaction records, notifications, reconciliation and exception desk. Can submit payments and linked refunds within partner-granted limits. Owns detection, escalation and merchant communication during its service incidents. | Payment and refund IDs, verified provider references, status history, device audit, statement-to-payment links and outstanding exceptions. |
| Acquirer and contracted processor | Acquirer signs and underwrites Mimi for each channel, assigns or approves merchant and terminal identifiers, sets acceptance terms, handles scheme access and funds Mimi. Processor can host checkout, operate the terminal host and route financial messages under delegated authority. Acquirer owns beneficiary verification, reserves and chargeback debits under the contract. | Merchant and terminal approvals, authoritative transaction status, itemized remittances, fees and adjustments, dispute notices and payout references. |
| Issuer, scheme and settlement institutions | Issuer or authorized stand-in makes the authorization decision. The scheme routes messages and determines interbank obligations through clearing. Participating institutions settle those obligations under the rail’s arrangements. Mimi’s bank posts the separate merchant deposit. | Authorization and clearing evidence available through the processor, then independent bank posting available to Mimi. |

dakim pay’s credentials exclude bank-detail edits, payout initiation, arbitrary transfers and independent refund destinations. Mimi makes beneficiary changes through the acquirer’s verified channel. If the partner cannot enforce that separation, select a different access model before connecting the account. Onboarding software can collect or refer a case with permission. The acquirer’s approval for the exact merchant and channels gates activation. Agreements must identify the owner of underwriting, fraud losses, dispute deadlines and financial-crime decisions. A jurisdictional review must also establish obligations that apply independently to dakim pay. ## What dakim pay builds

- **Independent service.** Use one Go application with API and worker processes and its own PostgreSQL database, deployment, secrets, staff identities and backups. Separate sandbox and live credentials. Authenticate each merchant and scope every order, reader, payment, refund, report and support action to its merchant. Mimi’s POS keeps a replaceable adapter. Its online storefront adds a server-side checkout client. Neither company writes the other’s database.
- **Merchant contract.** Proposed endpoints are `POST /v1/payments`, `GET /v1/payments/{id}`, `POST /v1/payments/{id}/cancel`, `POST /v1/payments/{id}/refunds` and read-only payout detail. Creation takes a merchant order/tender reference, channel, integer minor amount and currency, plus an enrolled terminal ID or an allowed checkout return destination. It returns a stable payment ID and either terminal status or a short-lived partner-hosted checkout URL. Mimi can recover a lost create response by replaying its durable key. Its local store can poll outbound, so the till can operate behind the store firewall.
- **Durable transactions.** Persist the request and a dispatch record before contacting the partner. Bind each idempotency key to merchant, operation and payload. A matching replay returns the original resource, a changed payload fails, and the tender reference prevents a second collection while the original is unresolved or already captured. Replacing a definitively failed attempt requires an explicit new attempt linked to the same tender. Preserve partner operation and charge references. Serialize concurrent refunds against refundable value, including pending refunds, and surface chargeback overlap. Record payments, refunds, disputes, statements and bank matches separately. A captured payment can later have a dispute and a returned payout simultaneously.
- **Authenticated notifications.** Verify upstream notifications, persist before acknowledging, deduplicate by provider account and event ID, and inspect the original transaction when facts conflict or arrive out of order. Publish signed, replayable merchant events for online clients. Consumers deduplicate and re-read payment status. Retain evidence under an agreed retention policy. Card number, security code and PIN entry stay inside the approved reader or partner-hosted page. Keep those values out of APIs, support tickets and logs. [Provider webhook behaviour](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks) · [PCI SSC on shared responsibility](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/does-pci-dss-apply-to-merchants-who-outsource-all-payment-processing-operations-and-never-store-process-or-transmit-cardholder-data/)
- **Merchant operations.** Manage terminal serial number, store, lane, partner terminal ID, firmware and assignment history. Provide payment search, an unknown-payment queue, terminal replacement, finance reports and tracked dispute cases. Support can inspect a redacted timeline. Financial actions require merchant authority, audit and elevated approval according to risk. The partner or its approved device service owns key injection, terminal cryptographic material and approved firmware distribution under the proposed contract. dakim pay invoices its subscriptions, devices and support in its own accounts.

## Terminal and online traces

| Stage | Staffed terminal · T-01 · USD 42.00 | Online checkout · W-01 · USD 68.00 |
|---|---|---|
| Request | Cashier confirms the total. Mimi freezes the tender and sends its operation key and enrolled lane. dakim pay records T-01 and dispatches the amount to the mapped terminal through the partner. | Mimi’s server freezes the priced order and requests W-01. dakim pay creates a partner-hosted checkout session bound to that order, merchant, amount and currency. The customer follows the returned link. |
| Authenticate and authorize | The customer taps or inserts the card at the reader and completes the requested verification. The processor sends the authorization through the acquirer and scheme to the issuer. The answer returns on that chain. | The customer enters card details on the partner’s page. The partner invokes issuer authentication such as 3-D Secure when applicable. Authentication and the subsequent issuer authorization are distinct results. |
| Capture and fulfill | Under the proposed automatic-capture profile, dakim pay verifies successful capture against the original reference, merchant, terminal, amount and currency. Mimi records the tender and issues one receipt. A reader approval followed by a connection loss leaves the lane in “Checking payment.” | The partner processes capture and sends an authenticated server notification. dakim pay verifies the payment facts and exposes the result to Mimi. Mimi marks the order paid and releases it to fulfillment once. The browser return displays status. A shopper can close the tab and still have the order completed. |
| Clear | The processor presents the captured $42.00 for clearing. The acquirer reports its transaction line, costs and payout allocation. | The $68.00 capture clears. Mimi later approves a $10.00 partial refund. dakim pay submits R-01 against W-01. The partner confirms the original-card refund and includes the debit in this example’s payout. |
| Fund and match | The terminal capture and its $0.84 fee contribute **$41.16** to the payout. | The web capture, refund and $1.70 fee contribute **$56.30**. Mimi matches one **$97.46** posted deposit to the partner payout below. |

For an unresolved terminal attempt, poll T-01 and inspect its original partner transaction. After a dispatch timeout, reuse the partner’s original idempotency reference within its supported replay window. If that window has expired and lookup is inconclusive, escalate for processor evidence. Hold substitute tenders on this basket until cancellation, decline or the financial outcome is verified. A failed status query keeps the case open. Offline card acceptance is disabled in this release. Once capture is confirmed, a cancellation request is resolved under the partner’s rules and any required refund is a distinct financial event. Other customers can use another available lane. Apply the same recovery rule to an expired web session that may already have captured. ## How finance proves the deposit

Assume the partner’s sample statement for merchant M-01 explicitly assigns both channels to payout P-01 in USD. Fees below are illustrative statement amounts. The partner’s cutoff, timezone and channel-specific merchant IDs determine real membership of a batch.

| Partner line | Original operation | Effect on P-01 (USD) |
|---|---|---:|
| Terminal capture | T-01 | +42.00 |
| Online capture | W-01 | +68.00 |
| Online partial refund | R-01 against W-01 | −10.00 |
| Terminal processing fee | T-01 | −0.84 |
| Online processing fee | W-01 | −1.70 |
| **Partner net payout P-01** | **Bank trace B-01** | **97.46** |

1. **Mimi tenders against dakim pay.** Match both captured tenders and the refund by merchant, original references, currency and amount. Record each exactly once. Missing retail receipts after capture go to Mimi’s exception queue. dakim pay can notify the merchant of an unmatched capture but cannot issue a grocery receipt on its behalf.
2. **dakim pay records against the acquirer.** Match each gross transaction, fee and adjustment to a unique source statement line. Deduplicate reimports, preserve corrections as dated entries, and independently review unresolved amounts. The partner instructs the payout. Its report establishes which items contributed to the net. A partner “sent” status opens a deposit-in-transit item. [Example itemized payout report](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/payout-reconciliation)
3. **Partner payout against Mimi’s bank.** Mimi finance obtains the posted bank entry independently and matches B-01, the verified account, currency and $97.46 amount. An amount-only coincidence, missing reference, returned transfer or missing bank entry stays open. If the partner pays the channels separately, match the actual payout and bank references for each. When evidence is aggregate, keep the unexplained allocation visible until the partner provides detail. A later chargeback or bank return creates a new dated debit, linked to the original capture or payout. The earlier matched deposit remains in the history.

In Mimi’s illustrative books, captures debit card clearing $110.00 against the appropriate retail tender postings. The confirmed refund credits clearing $10.00 against its return posting. When the partner remits, debit deposits in transit $97.46 and fee expense $2.54, and credit clearing $100.00. At the bank posting, debit bank $97.46 and credit deposits in transit $97.46. Clearing and transit both finish at zero. Sales tax and revenue recognition follow Mimi’s retail accounting. dakim pay records its separately billed service revenue and expenses. The $110.00 of grocery collections never enters its bank account.

## What it takes to go live

| Gate and accountable owner | Evidence required for release |
|---|---|
| Market and contracts · dakim pay leadership, qualified local adviser, acquirer and Mimi | Choose country, entities, currency and funds flow. Obtain direct merchant approval for both channels and the supplier appointment. Set fees, support, refund and dispute rights, liability, required registration or local permissions, sponsor escalation and termination terms. Verify who can change the bank beneficiary. Validate the service margin after equipment, replacement, support and contracted losses. [Visa third-party agent registration](https://partner.visa.com/site/programs/third-party-agent-registration.html) |
| Device and online acceptance · acquirer, terminal partner and dakim pay | Obtain partner acceptance for the exact reader/application/firmware/host combination, applicable test or inherited approval evidence, deployment permissions, hosted checkout and channel limits. Commission inventory, provisioning, tamper response, spare swap and old-device revocation. Test online authentication challenge, decline, browser abandonment and late notification. Record which tests the participant system requires. [EMVCo Level 3 testing](https://www.emvco.com/emv-technologies/emv-level-3-testing/) |
| Security and risk · each company’s security lead and the acquirer | Agree PCI validation and responsibilities against actual data flows, including web redirects, secrets, terminal support and incident access. Verify merchant isolation, credential rotation, authentic notifications and resource-level authorization. Assign fraud review, online velocity controls, customer remediation, acquirer escalation, financial-crime responsibilities and dispute evidence deadlines. [PCI SSC on outsourced processing](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/does-pci-dss-apply-to-merchants-who-outsource-all-payment-processing-operations-and-never-store-process-or-transmit-cardholder-data/) |
| Reliability and reconciliation · dakim pay operations, partner support and Mimi finance | Agree operating hours, response times, age limits for unknown transactions and missing deposits, recovery objectives, funding calendar and named escalation contacts. Rehearse reader power loss after authorization, lost capture reply, simultaneous refund requests, late disputes, duplicate files, negative payouts and bank returns. Restore from backup, fence the previous writer, recover intervening partner events and reconcile before resuming dispatch. Rehearse out-of-hours support with the actual coverage team. |
| Authorized pilot and release · acquirer, Mimi and dakim pay | First run synthetic tests and the partner sandbox. Under written partner authorization, commission the physical reader and online checkout with permitted transactions. Retain evidence of a capture and original-card refund on each channel, matching statement lines and posted bank entries across applicable payout cycles. Observe a device swap and recovery after a lost response. Assign every exception, clear unexplained differences for the pilot sample and obtain sign-off from Mimi finance and the operating teams before expansion. |

During an incident, suspend new acceptance for the affected channel while preserving status queries, reconciliation and support for outstanding transactions. A rollback or merchant exit must keep original-reference refunds, dispute deadlines, historical statements and support access operable for the agreed period. Before a second merchant goes live, run the same contract tests from an unrelated POS, verify cross-merchant isolation and measure support cost per active terminal. Direct acquiring, merchant balances, wallet issuance and discretionary payouts would each require a fresh business, authority and funds-flow decision.
