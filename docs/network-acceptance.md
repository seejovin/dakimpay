# Accepting Visa, Mastercard and American Express

The current brand selector chooses a synthetic instrument in the local acquirer. All six brand/channel combinations run through the application. A successful test establishes dakim pay’s transaction behaviour. Live eligibility is specific to the merchant, country, currency, channel, acquiring agreement and device configuration.

## One partner integration with three verified acceptance paths

Mimi signs a direct merchant agreement with an acquiring partner that can service its country and business. Request written support for **each network in each channel**. Ask who underwrites Mimi, who provides the terminal and its software, which processor operates the hosted checkout, which party handles disputes and who sends each merchant payout. Some arrangements use different merchant IDs, bank descriptors or statements for different brands. Implement the actual mapping the partner provides and obtain a sample of every remittance format.

Use an existing approved reader and partner-hosted checkout. Ask the acquirer which terminal/device approvals are eligible for reuse and which end-to-end Level 3 tests its participant system requires. Issuer authentication, authorization, capture, clearing, payout and bank posting are distinct evidence points. Confirm PCI responsibilities for the deployment, merchant site, redirects, support systems and partner relationships with the acquirer and the appropriate assessor. [EMVCo Level 3 testing](https://www.emvco.com/emv-technologies/emv-level-3-testing/) · [PCI SSC on outsourced processing](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/does-pci-dss-apply-to-merchants-who-outsource-all-payment-processing-operations-and-never-store-process-or-transmit-cardholder-data/)

## Partner acceptance evidence

1. Record a row per **network × channel** with country, currency, merchant identifiers, partner account, approved reader/firmware or checkout version, responsible institution, and required approvals. Keep unavailable combinations disabled.
2. Replace the emulator with an account-bound connector using the partner’s provided credentials, approval device or test cards, API specifications, idempotency rules and signed notifications/status queries. Record timeout and network-failure outcomes. Enforce maximum refund against the original capture.
3. Run partner sandbox and hardware test cases for approvals, declines, lost capture replies, online challenges, refunds, duplicate delivery, fee lines, disputes and failed or late payouts. Verify that bank beneficiary instructions remain outside dakim pay’s authority.
4. Under partner authorization, commission the exact merchant/reader/site and online domain. Finance must reconcile each enabled brand and channel from original capture to the partner remittance and independently observed bank entry, across actual funding cycles. Get partner, merchant finance, security and operations sign-off before advertising acceptance. Track pending or restricted combinations separately.
